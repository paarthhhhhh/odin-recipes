Paneer Tikka

Description

Paneer tikka is a popular Indian appetizer made from cubes of paneer (a soft, fresh cheese) that are marinated in a spiced yogurt mixture and then grilled, baked, or pan-seared until lightly charred at the edges. The marinade typically includes yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, and a blend of spices like red chili powder, turmeric, garam masala, cumin, and coriander, along with a bit of lemon juice and mustard or gram flour (besan) to help it cling to the paneer and create a nice sear.

Ingredients

  • 250g paneer, cut into cubes
  • 1/2 cup thick yogurt (curd)
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1 tbsp gram flour (besan) or mustard oil — helps binding and adds flavor
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Salt, to taste
  • 1–2 tbsp oil (for basting/grilling)
  • 1 onion, cut into chunks
  • 1 bell pepper (capsicum), cut into chunks
  • 1 tomato, cut into chunks (optional, holds up less well on grill)
  • Chaat masala (sprinkled on top after cooking)
  • Lemon wedges
  • Fresh coriander leaves, chopped

Steps

  1. Prepare the marinade – In a bowl, mix yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, red chili powder, turmeric, garam masala, cumin powder, coriander powder, gram flour (or mustard oil), lemon juice, and salt. Whisk until smooth.
  2. Marinate the paneer – Add the paneer cubes (along with onion and bell pepper chunks, if using) to the marinade. Gently toss to coat everything evenly. Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes (1–2 hours for deeper flavor).
  3. Prep the skewers – If using wooden skewers, soak them in water for 15–20 minutes beforehand to prevent burning. Thread the marinated paneer, onion, and bell pepper alternately onto the skewers.
  4. Preheat your cooking surface – Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F), or heat a grill/tawa (griddle) on medium-high heat.
  5. Cook the tikka
    • Oven: Place skewers on a baking tray lined with foil and bake for 15–20 minutes, turning halfway through, until edges are lightly charred.
    • Grill/Tandoor: Grill for 8–10 minutes, turning occasionally, until charred spots appear.
    • Stovetop (tawa/pan): Pan-sear on medium-high heat with a little oil, turning occasionally, for about 8–10 minutes until browned and slightly crisp.
  6. Baste while cooking – Brush the skewers with a little oil or melted butter once or twice during cooking to keep the paneer moist and help it char nicely.
  7. Finish and garnish – Once cooked, remove from heat and sprinkle with chaat masala and a squeeze of fresh lemon juice.
  8. Serve – Serve hot with mint or coriander chutney, sliced onions, and lemon wedges on the side.
