Shakshuka

Description

Shakshuka is a popular North African and Middle Eastern dish made of eggs poached in a rich, spiced tomato and pepper sauce. It's typically eaten for breakfast or brunch, though it's hearty enough to serve any time of day. The base sauce is made by sautéing onions, garlic, and bell peppers, then simmering them with crushed tomatoes and a blend of warm spices — commonly cumin, paprika, and sometimes chili powder or cayenne for heat. The sauce is cooked down until thick and fragrant.

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 3–4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika (sweet or smoked)
  • 1/2 tsp chili powder or cayenne (adjust to taste)
  • 1 can (400g / 14oz) crushed tomatoes
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 1 tsp sugar (optional, balances acidity)
  • 5–6 eggs
  • Fresh parsley or cilantro, chopped
  • Crumbled feta cheese (optional)

Steps

  1. Sauté the aromatics – Heat olive oil in a large skillet or pan over medium heat. Add the diced onion and bell pepper, and cook for 5–7 minutes until softened.
  2. Add garlic and spices – Stir in the minced garlic, cumin, paprika, and chili powder. Cook for another minute until fragrant.
  3. Add the tomatoes – Pour in the crushed tomatoes, season with salt, pepper, and sugar (if using). Stir well and let the sauce simmer for 10–15 minutes, until it thickens slightly.
  4. Make wells for the eggs – Using a spoon, create small wells in the sauce, spacing them evenly around the pan.
  5. Add the eggs – Crack an egg directly into each well.
  6. Cover and cook – Cover the pan with a lid and cook on low-medium heat for 5–8 minutes, until the egg whites are set but the yolks are still soft and runny (cook longer if you prefer firmer yolks).
  7. Garnish – Remove from heat and sprinkle with chopped parsley or cilantro, and crumbled feta if desired.
  8. Serve – Serve hot, straight from the pan, with crusty bread, pita, or challah for scooping up the sauce and eggs.
