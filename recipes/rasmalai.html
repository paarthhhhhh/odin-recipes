Rasmalai

Description

Rasmalai is a popular Indian dessert made of soft, spongy cheese patties soaked in a rich, chilled, sweetened milk that's flavored with cardamom and saffron.

Ingredients

  • 2 liter full-fat milk
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice or vinegar (to curdle the milk)
  • 1 tbsp all-purpose flour or cornstarch (optional, helps binding)
  • 1.5 cups sugar
  • 6 cups water
  • 1/2 cup sugar (adjust to taste)
  • 1/4 tsp cardamom powder
  • A few strands of saffron
  • 8–10 pistachios, chopped (for garnish)
  • 8–10 almonds, chopped or slivered (for garnish)
  • 1 tbsp rose water (optional, for extra fragrance)

Steps

  1. Curdle the milk – Bring the milk to a boil in a heavy-bottomed pot. Once boiling, reduce heat and slowly add lemon juice or vinegar while stirring, until the milk curdles and separates into curds (chenna) and whey (greenish liquid).
  2. Strain and rinse – Pour the curdled milk through a muslin cloth or cheesecloth to strain out the whey. Rinse the chenna under cold water to remove any sourness from the lemon/vinegar.
  3. Drain and knead – Squeeze out excess water from the cloth, then hang it for 30 minutes to drain further. Transfer the chenna to a plate and knead it with your palms for 8–10 minutes until smooth and soft (no graininess). Mix in the flour/cornstarch if using.
  4. Shape the patties – Divide the chenna into small equal portions and roll each into a smooth ball, then gently flatten into small discs (they'll puff up while cooking).
  5. Prepare the sugar syrup – In a wide, deep pot, bring sugar and water to a boil until the sugar dissolves completely.
  6. Cook the patties – Gently drop the chenna discs into the boiling syrup. Cover and cook on medium-high heat for 15–20 minutes, until the discs double in size and become spongy. Avoid opening the lid too often.
  7. Cool the patties – Once cooked, remove the patties from the syrup and let them cool. Gently squeeze each one between your palms to remove excess syrup.
  8. Make the rabri – In a separate pot, simmer the milk on low-medium heat, stirring occasionally, until it reduces to about half its volume and thickens slightly. Add sugar, cardamom powder, and saffron strands. Simmer for another 5 minutes.
  9. Soak the patties – Add the squeezed chenna patties into the warm rabri, letting them soak and absorb the flavored milk. Let it cool to room temperature.
  10. Chill and garnish – Refrigerate for at least 2–3 hours (or overnight for best results). Before serving, garnish with chopped pistachios, almonds, and a few drops of rose water if desired.
  11. Serve – Serve chilled, either as is or with an extra drizzle of rabri on top.
